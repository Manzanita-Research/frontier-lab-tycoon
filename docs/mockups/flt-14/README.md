# FLT-14 skin mockups

Static HTML/CSS mockups by the FLT lead (Opus 5.5), rendered over the same photo-mode frame (`bg.png`). They are the visual reference for the skins. Open an `.html` file in a browser, or look at the `.jpg`.

| Skin | Mockup | Round | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frontier 95** (default) | `frontier-95.html` | 2 | Jem's pick. Add Discovery Disc's clip-art stickers and the Rest/Steady/Strenuous/Grueling pace labels |
| Discovery Disc '96 | `discovery-disc-96.html` | 2 | ships as a skin |
| GeoCities | `geocities.html` | 2 | ships as a skin |
| Swag Drop | `swag-drop.html` | 1 | ships as a skin |
| Karaoke Night | `karaoke-night.html` | 1 | ships as a skin (watch Pixelify's 5/S ambiguity; prefer Jersey 10 for digits) |
| Field Almanac | `field-almanac.html` | 1 | ships as a skin |

`type-sheet.*` compares Chalmers (paid, not bought) with free pixel faces. Fonts in the mockups load from Google Fonts / cdnfonts; skins must **bundle** their fonts (OFL files with licence) and never hot-link.
