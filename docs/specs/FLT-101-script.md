# FLT-101: Maximally Effective Altruists, the script

Every line the mod can show, for Jem to read before it ships. An opt-in example mod: nothing changes unless the link
carries `?mod=/mods/examples/maximally-effective-altruists/mod.json` (or it's added from Add/Remove Mods).

## The brief, and how this keeps to it

- **It's about the culture, and the comedy is the scheduling.** The jokes land on EV spreadsheets, rotas for the rota,
  longtermist dating on a 500-year horizon, a manor bought "for convening", a Hot Tub filed as wellbeing infrastructure,
  and a leaked Sankey chart that weighs a weekend in expected hugs. Everyone in it is a consenting, cheerfully
  over-organised adult.
- **No jokes about harassment, consent, or anyone who got hurt.** None of the lines touch them. Re-read on the final
  pass, with one cut: Dashiell's "Treasurer (Interim)" became "Head of Spreadsheets", because an EA chart with a
  treasurer on it points at a real scandal and the real people in it.
- **PG-13.** Innuendo and fade-to-black only: the manor's lights go off at 11:04pm and the camera fades, a door closes
  (a sound), and a sock hangs on the Cluster door. Nothing is shown. The Sankey is abstract bands between first names,
  breakout sessions and rooms.
- **Parody names only.** Utilsbury Manor, the Maximally Effective Altruists and every first name are invented. The
  parody scanner (`src/content/parody.test.ts`) now reads `mods/examples/**` and fails on the movement's real people,
  charities, houses and forums. The Drama lint denylist (`drama/denylist.json`) has them too, for the daily packs.
- **The Senate question is a ticker headline only.** The Hearing pack (FLT-52) isn't moddable yet: its questions are code,
  not a content section.
